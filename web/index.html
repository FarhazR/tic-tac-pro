Tic-Tac-Pro

Three symbols each. Place a fourth and your oldest one vanishes.

How to play
  1. Take turns placing your symbol in an empty cell.
  2. You can only have three symbols on the board. Your fourth move removes your oldest one.
  3. The win is checked after the removal, so a line can disappear as you make it.
  4. The dashed cell is your oldest symbol. It is the one that expires next.
